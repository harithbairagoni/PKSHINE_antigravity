Corporate Governance FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Key operational details on onboarding, data confidentiality, and engagement governance.

How quickly can technical specialists or project pods be deployed? +
For standard IT staffing and resource augmentation, pre-vetted engineers are typically ready for client review within 48 to 72 hours, with onboarding completed within 5 to 10 business days. For turnkey fixed-scope projects, project kickoff follows brief architectural discovery and SLA agreement.
How does Peacock Shine protect client IP and commercial confidentiality? +
All engagements are governed by strict bilateral Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) executed prior to any technical disclosure. All source code, architectural artifacts, and proprietary data produced under contract remain the 100% exclusive intellectual property of the client organization.
What contract and billing structures do you support? +
We offer transparent, flexible commercial models tailored to project scope: Time & Material (T&M) for agile teams, Fixed-Price Milestone Delivery for turnkey projects, and Dedicated Monthly FTE Retainers for managed Offshore Development Centers (ODC).
Do you serve domestic clients across India and international clients globally? +
Yes. Headquartered in Karnataka, Peacock Shine operates a hybrid delivery model serving enterprise clients across India as well as international partners in North America, Europe, and the APAC region with multi-timezone support and SLA coverage.
