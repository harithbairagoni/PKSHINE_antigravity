Who We Are

Peacock Shine Private Limited is a professional consulting and technology solutions company focused on delivering innovative, reliable and cost-effective business solutions.

Our mission is to help organizations achieve their strategic objectives through professional consulting, technology services, project execution and business transformation.

We believe in building long-term relationships through integrity, operational excellence and a commitment to client success.

As a full-spectrum consulting organization, we provide the strategic foresight and technical execution capacity necessary to navigate modern market complexities. We serve clients across Banking, Telecom, Healthcare, Information Technology, and are expanding into Infrastructure and Engineering verticals.
